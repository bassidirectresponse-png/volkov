# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in Brazil and international markets who want clear, responsible context
before making health and wellness decisions. Visitors may be researching
ingredients, supplement labels, commercial relationships, or questions to take
to a qualified healthcare professional.

## Product Purpose

VOLKOV is the institutional and editorial home of VOLKOV LTDA. It establishes
the company as a transparent Brazilian digital publisher and performance
marketing business, explains its research standards, publishes practical
consumer education, discloses affiliate relationships, and provides direct
human contact. Success means visitors can understand who VOLKOV is, how its
content is produced, where commercial incentives exist, and what the limits of
the information are.

## Brand Personality

Clear, sober, and quietly authoritative. The experience should feel like a
carefully assembled research dossier: cinematic and expressive without
sensationalism, institutional without pretending to be medical, and premium
without becoming exclusive or obscure.

## Anti-references

- Aggressive direct-response funnels, fake urgency, or exaggerated promises
- Generic supplement stores and dropshipping templates
- Fake clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, hospitals, or medical news portals
- Startup-template layouts, fabricated social proof, ratings, awards, or seals
- Undisclosed affiliate marketing or implied official merchant relationships
- Celebrity imagery, white coats, before-and-after imagery, or fictitious products

## Design Principles

1. Make clarity visible: structure evidence, limitations, warnings, and disclosures
   so they are easy to scan and difficult to miss.
2. Earn trust through specificity: use real company details, concrete editorial
   practices, working contact paths, and honest empty states.
3. Separate education from commerce: commercial relationships must be disclosed
   near relevant actions without changing editorial conclusions.
4. Create distinction through restraint: bold typography, asymmetric composition,
   and purposeful motion should support comprehension rather than compete with it.
5. Design for durability: reusable typed content and components should allow the
   publication to expand without weakening consistency or compliance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast,
semantic structure, 44px touch targets, zoom, screen readers, reduced motion,
static fallbacks for animated sections, and balanced consent choices. Do not
block selection, copying, context menus, or native scrolling.
